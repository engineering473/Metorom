# Metorom product context

Metorom is an independent audio design project based in Kyoto. Its current site presents a three-way loudspeaker/monitor as an evolving object to hear, inspect, measure, and discuss.

## Confirmed product facts from the existing site

- Three acoustic paths use an Alpha 6A midrange driver, BMS 4540ND compression driver and horn, and LAB 12 low-frequency driver.
- The system is modular and its DSP tuning can evolve.
- The current site names 608 mm midrange spacing and 510 µs DSP latency.
- Frequency response and horizontal directivity measurements appear in the existing site, with REW named in the measurement copy.
- The Silk Road serves as a metaphor for exchanging ideas across engineering, craft, music, and daily life. It is a design philosophy, not a geographic product claim.
- A listening session is in planning. Date, venue, ticket price, and accessibility details have not been confirmed. Concept pages must not invent them.

## Audience and purpose

The site addresses listeners, designers, and potential collaborators who want to understand the speaker and its design philosophy before joining a future listening event. The concept studies should help the owner compare visual directions while keeping the product truth and wayfinding clear.

## Current design task

Five site families, each with an Impeccable and a Taste study. The ten studies are alternatives, not a blended design system. Use the 15 local Pinterest screenshots documented in `design/inspiration/pinterest/site-families.md` and the owner's 21st bookmarks as composition and UI references. Hero imagery is intentionally deferred: every site reserves its media position with a flat CSS placeholder, and the studies include no newly generated or sourced imagery.

Existing interactive versions in `versions/atelier`, `versions/signal`, and `versions/journey` remain separate, functional explorations of the 3D viewer, bilingual content, scroll progress, and event flow.
