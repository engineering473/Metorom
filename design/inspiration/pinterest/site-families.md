# Fifteen sites, five aesthetic families

A sorted collection of website and layout snapshots. Each family lists the features its members share, followed by a short note on every snapshot.

## Monochrome product minimal

One object, lots of empty space. These pages let a single product or a single idea fill a white or pale grey canvas and trust thin type to do the rest.

**Key features**

- White or light grey grounds with black type
- A lone hero object photographed cleanly, often cropped by the page edge
- Light-weight grotesque headings and tiny grey body text
- Hairline rules, spec tables and outlined buttons instead of filled ones
- Oversized wordmarks or headlines used as the graphic

**Snapshots**

### Infinite Machine P1

An electric scooter site that opens on the angular vehicle alone, with two colour dots and one outlined "Configure Now" button. Top speed and range sit in a ruled table that reads like a gauge, a motion-blurred photograph makes speed feel physical, and close-up crops of the motor and handlebar finish the page.

### Opal Tadpole

A pale grey hero shows the webcam standing vertically like a sculpture, with one blue "Available now" link as the only colour. Three products sit in hairline-divided columns, and a gigantic black "Opal" wordmark runs off the edge as the footer.

### Financial Goals

A fintech landing page split down the middle, black on the left and white on the right. Glossy black 3D forms and floating balance cards give it depth, and the sections alternate light panels with dark stat cards while a slim progress line tracks your place.

## Warm architectural editorial

Interior, architecture and materials brands that want to feel calm and expensive. Photography leads, colour is borrowed from wood, plaster and stone, and the layout is spaced like a printed monograph.

**Key features**

- Cream and beige backgrounds with deep brown or charcoal text
- Full-bleed photography of real rooms and buildings
- Very large, tightly set sans headings, sometimes laid over the image
- Ruled grids for statistics and small labels in a side column
- Accordions and plus icons for projects and services

**Snapshots**

### Autex Acoustics

A cream page that opens on a timber-lined ceiling and moves through oversized "Solutions" and "Projects" headings, each with a plus toggle. Project rows expand into photo pairs with product tags, and a dark story section closes with large circles.

### Oura & Co.

An interior design agency whose hero shows a blurred figure walking through a warm room, under a huge white headline that overlaps the photo. Below, a beige panel presents its numbers in ruled cells, with one solid chocolate cell for emphasis.

### Forma Studio

An architecture practice with a large, light "Vision — Precision" headline above a wide landscape render. It uses a statistics row, a charcoal project list where inactive titles fade back, a client-logo wall and an expandable list of services.

## Colour-led tile catalogue

Retail brands built around one dominant hue. Products are shown as cut-outs on neutral tiles, and the grid of tiles is the layout.

**Key features**

- One strong brand colour, rust or amber, repeated in type and photography
- Modular tile grids mixing product cut-outs, lifestyle shots and colour panels
- Big headings that act as a wordmark
- Small rounded or underlined calls to action
- Plain footers and adaptive layouts shown across devices

**Snapshots**

### Madeni

A furniture brand whose enormous terracotta wordmark spans the width and is overlapped by a hero product. Grey tiles hold each piece, a lifestyle photograph repeats the same rust tones, and the mobile and tablet mockups at the bottom show the layout adapting.

### GRAU

A lighting brand framed in black, with amber-lit photography of people at home. Pale product panels sit beside a full orange gradient tile in a rounded grid, each with a small white "Learn more" pill, and the page closes on a plain black footer.

## Soft gradient haze

Interfaces where light is the decoration. Blurred colour washes replace illustration, so software and research content feel airy and modern without needing screenshots.

**Key features**

- Large, heavily blurred gradients in blue, pink, orange or purple
- Frosted or dark glass panels with thin borders
- Pill badges and pill buttons
- Centred, light-weight headlines, sometimes mixed with an italic serif
- Big statistics presented in ruled or boxed grids

**Snapshots**

### Aurion

A SaaS blog page on near-black navy, with a glowing blue band across the hero that looks like a horizon. Small badges flanked by fine lines label each section, and articles sit in dark glass panels with a white "Read more" pill.

### Enblox

A productivity app landing page on white, with a pink and orange haze behind a hand holding a phone. The bold headline mixes in an italic serif phrase, three short feature columns follow, and a full-width purple-to-orange photograph closes the section.

### Food market research deck

A set of Russian-language presentation slides about the packaged food market, on navy gradients with misty edges. Large figures such as 5.7%, 11.7%, 18.1% and 13.3% sit in ruled boxes with crop-mark corners, and a small table pairs each quarter with a bar indicator.

## Dark tech and cinematic HUD

Black interfaces that borrow from gadgets, games and film titles. They favour drama and detail, with glowing accents, annotations and layered type.

**Key features**

- Black or charcoal backgrounds with one sharp accent, yellow, green or orange
- Glow, grain and blur used as lighting
- Oversized or overlapping type, sometimes translucent
- Callout lines, bounding boxes and small data readouts
- Outlined panels, pill tabs and thin progress bars

**Snapshots**

### SPHR

A sapphire-glass brand page with a huge translucent wordmark crossed by acid-yellow lines. Pill tabs, outlined rounded panels, a small circular selector and staggered headings that overlap make it feel like a control panel.

### HAOS Tech Solutions

A brand identity board on black, with a soft green ring glowing through vertical stripes. Tiny metadata columns run across the top, the title is set in light sans, and a thin progress bar and a glowing app icon finish the frame.

### Krola Lord Buds

A product poster for earbuds, with a blurred macro shot lit in orange and teal. A selection box with handles and leader-line callouts mimics a design tool, and a hairline table at the bottom lists the product number and copy.

### National Geographic redesign

A charcoal, grain-textured concept for a science and exploration site. Yellow is used only for the logo and buttons, moon and planet imagery bleeds off the edges, and ruled article grids, a torn-paper divider and phone screens show its series, galleries and maps.
