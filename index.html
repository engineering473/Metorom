About the project

A three-way monitor,
designed from the driver up.

Dual midranges split by a passive crossover to kill comb filtering, a horn-loaded compression tweeter, and a 12" driver handling the low end — all tuned in REW and stitched together with active DSP.

3

drivers per side

608mm

midrange spacing

510µs

DSP latency

Built to be taken apart.

Every driver mounts independently, the crossover is swappable, and the DSP profile is field-tunable — modularity and adjustability aren't afterthoughts here, they're the design brief.

Where the road ends

We draw from the Silk Road.

Its overland routes are usually mapped as ending in Chang'an — but the goods kept moving. Carried onward by Japanese missions to Tang China, Persian glassware and Central Asian textiles reached the imperial storehouses of Nara. The Shōsōin repository still holds them today — proof that this corner of Japan was, over a thousand years ago, already connected to the other end of the continent.
