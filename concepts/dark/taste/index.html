MEASURE 01Frequency response

REW measurements show the on-axis response and the contribution of each driver. Where the curves meet, the crossover regions become visible. The graph guides tuning; the room remains the final test.

MEASURE 02Horizontal directivity

A change in angle changes what reaches the listener. Mapping energy across angle and frequency helps reveal how the speaker behaves beyond one listening position.

METHOD 03Listen, adjust, repeat

Measured behavior and personal listening stay in dialogue. Neither replaces the other; both give the next revision a clearer direction.
