THE SYSTEMACOUSTIC ARCHITECTURE

Separate paths.
Shared purpose.

The horn, midrange drivers, and low-frequency cabinet each do a specific job. The modular structure leaves room for adjustment as listening and measurement inform the next revision.

01BMS 4540NDHigh frequency / horn

A compression driver and horn help control high-frequency coverage. The aim is consistent listening beyond one fixed position.

02Alpha 6ADedicated midrange

A separate acoustic path gives voices and instruments space. Midrange spacing is 608 mm in the current arrangement.

03LAB 12Low-frequency foundation

The low-frequency driver anchors the three-way system while the other acoustic paths retain their own roles.
