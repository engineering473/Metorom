# Metorom v3

The October 28–29, 2026 revision of the sculptural site. The previous version and every earlier study remain in source. Home renders directly at `/`, with Philosophy, Contact and Event at `/philosophy/`, `/contact/` and `/event/`. The production build preserves the old v2 and v3 page URLs as compatibility redirects to these clean public paths, retaining query parameters and deep links. Design details and specifications are integrated into Home; their previous standalone URLs redirect to the matching sections.

The visual direction uses deep blue (`#101d8f`), white, pale blue instrument surfaces and flat editorial transitions. The original floating capsule navbar and rounded gallery cards are preserved; other editorial sections use flat transitions. The original supplied product imagery remains intact.

Leveled listening has three permanently open driver explanations and comparable schematic sine waves. These illustrate pitch; they are not measurements or recordings. Divider lines, wave baselines and pause controls have been removed. Wave motion runs once for under five seconds and reduced-motion remains static. The illustrative audio charts use their full card width without count badges; the separate REW image plots have been removed from the page and published assets.

The A closer look coverflow section is removed from Home, the section index and the home editor. The eight-photo liquid-glass carousel now follows the text of Every element has a purpose. Its former Leveled Listening heading and visible photo titles are removed; accessible photo names and navigation remain. Philosophy precedes Keep the conversation open, which is the final section before the footer.

The speaker section follows the natural video height instead of a viewport-height minimum. A stable square frame matches the supplied poster and exploded clips; wide preview clips compensate only for their encoded letterboxing. This removes the abrupt CSS scale change and keeps all exploded parts visible. The on-video Subwoofer, Left midrange and Tweeter hotspots open their supplied exploded-view films and hold the final frame. Selecting a driver also fades in its corresponding illustrative response curve, using the same series as Measurements. Desktop places the graph in the left column beneath the object introduction; phones place it below the square video inside the black frame. The graph remains visible throughout closing and the reverse light fade, then fades away. Its visible data note distinguishes the model from a measured response; X or Escape plays the matching return film. The separate left-column control list and instructions are removed.

One six-row specification sheet covers the three-way system, 1 × LAB 12, 2 × Alpha 6A in parallel, 1 × BMS 4540ND with horn, 96 kHz FIR/parametric EQ and 798 × 600 × 340 mm dimensions (W × H × D). The long electronics tables, construction, spacing, latency and development details are removed. Square actions are solid blue on light surfaces and white on blue surfaces. Decorative divider lines are removed while the index rule and functional loading progress remain.

The loading screen uses the supplied Newton component. A shared queue prepares images, fonts and all fourteen animation clips, then reuses Blob URLs for playback. Progress counts completed resource tasks. A Continue control appears after five seconds, and a twenty-second deadline releases a stalled connection. Hero and poster WebP copies together are about 110 KB; their original PNGs are preserved.

The tweeter's revised exploded view uses the supplied `Tweeter-0355-0547.mp4`, split at its fully exploded source frame 96 (4 seconds). The opening and return clips share that endpoint, retain the source's 1920-square framing and 24 fps, and use versioned filenames to avoid stale cached media. The opening holds on its last frame until Close; the return still finishes with the existing reverse light fade.

The carousel initially centers Forest light (DSCF2928), photo 04/08, with a matching lazy placeholder. Its eight-photo order is preserved. On phones the driver response fades into a compact 172px card within the black model frame. A shorter SVG geometry keeps the axes readable, while the video retains its complete square framing and shrinks when needed to fit the available screen height. Landscape places the card beside the video. Opening or rotating the phone aligns the whole frame beneath the header; desktop and Measurements retain their existing full-size plots.

React, TypeScript, Tailwind, Motion, the `@/` alias and shadcn's `components/ui` structure were already configured. The supplied loader is in `components/ui/loader.tsx` with its easing helper and `lib/utils.ts`.

Use `npm run dev` for development, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run build`, and `node tools/check-v3-build.mjs` for validation. Preview the production output with `npm run preview`. The production base URL is `/` for the custom domain `www.metorom.com`; `public/CNAME` is copied into every build so publishing preserves the GitHub Pages domain setting.

Published copy is isolated in `public/content/sculptural-v3-*.json`. Preview editors use separate v3 browser storage. The owner can open `?edit=1` directly; visitors no longer see an editor or study chooser link in the footer.

The home introduction explains leveled listening, independent driver enclosures, and room tuning in three brief paragraphs. The introduction now includes the supplied liquid-glass carousel and the eight edited outdoor speaker photographs supplied on 5 November 2026. Full-composition WebP assets use -edited filenames in public/images/gallery-speakers; the previous thirteen photographs have been removed. Colors follow the supplied edits without a lighting overlay; 3:4 display crops use per-photo focal points without changing the originals. Wide paired photographs favor the complete right-hand speaker, while expanded view reveals the full photograph. The carousel loads near the viewport, pauses rendering offscreen, supports horizontal drag, previous/next controls, arrow keys, Enter/Space and Escape, and preserves vertical page scrolling. English/Japanese controls, reduced motion, and a non-WebGL image fallback are available. Three text buttons below retain the horn, midrange and bass details, schematic waves, design/material notes and original horn PDF. Opening a design detail does not expand the page or move its scroll position. Measurements retains its compact eight-row sheet and illustrative response charts. Philosophy retains its dedicated page and footer link. The preview editors and deep links remain available.

Confirmed materials are MDF for the main and midrange structures and locally sourced Japanese wood for the horn; both are sealed, primed, painted and clear-coated. No wood species or unverified performance rating is inferred. Page-copy loading shares only navigation and brand fields from home so each page keeps its own heading and introduction.
