Frequency response +

REW measurements show the on-axis response and each driver's contribution. The crossover regions appear where their curves meet; those readings guide the next listening and tuning pass.

Horizontal directivity +

Angular measurements show how sound energy changes with frequency away from the centre seat. They help us understand the room-facing behaviour of the speaker.
