03 / OPEN QUESTIONS

A design is a
conversation.

The Silk Road suggests a way to work: carry ideas between disciplines, test them, and leave the route open for what comes next. The speaker's modular construction makes that attitude physical.

01 Why a modular system?+

The system can change as useful ideas arrive. Parts and DSP tuning remain open to revision.

02 Why show the measurements?+

Measurements let visitors see part of the reasoning behind the sound. They guide listening rather than stand in for it.

03 What comes after the prototype?+

Further listening, measurement, and conversation. The upcoming session is one way to bring those threads together.
