02 / OUR THINKINGLISTEN ↔ MEASURE

A better answer

We listen first: to the room, the person, and the music. Then measurements help us see what the ear has already begun to notice.

The Silk Road is our metaphor for exchange. Ideas move between craft, engineering, music, and everyday life. A design becomes stronger when those routes remain open.

03 / FIELD NOTES

There is more
beneath the surface.

01Why three ways?+

Dedicated acoustic paths allow the high, middle, and low ranges to be considered on their own terms, then brought into balance as one speaker.

02Why keep the structure modular?+

Components and tuning can change as listening and measurement reveal a better solution. The design is built to evolve.

03What do measurements show?+

The existing project records frequency response and horizontal directivity. Those views guide tuning, but the listening experience remains the final question.
