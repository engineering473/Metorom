MEASUREMENT / 01Frequency responseOpen the measurement notes+

This REW plot shows the on-axis response and the contribution of each driver. The crossover regions are visible where their curves meet; these measurements guide listening and tuning rather than replacing them.

MEASUREMENT / 02Horizontal directivityOpen the measurement notes+

The color map shows how sound energy changes with angle and frequency. It helps us check that the speaker behaves predictably beyond a single listening point.

04 / A ROUTE OF IDEAS

A road made by exchange.

Follow the line. Each stop is a principle, not a literal travel itinerary.

01 / ORIGIN

Begin with listening.

A product begins with a person, a room, and a moment of attention. Those are the first design constraints.

02 / EXCHANGE

Let ideas meet.

The Silk Road inspires us as a network of exchange. Useful ideas can arrive from engineering, craft, music, and everyday life.

03 / EXPERIMENT

Test, tune, repeat.

Measurements reveal a pattern; listening gives it meaning. We move between the two until the object feels resolved.

04 / RETURN

Make room for change.

Modular parts and adjustable tuning let the system evolve. The end of one route can be the start of another.

05 / THE STUDIO
